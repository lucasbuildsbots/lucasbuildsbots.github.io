This site is both the code/CAD repository for this research and a guide for building a
passive dynamic walker (PDW) from nothing. It's built so you can go as deep as you
actually want and skip what you don't — pick one of the doors below.

## Where do you want to start?

### I don't know what a passive dynamic walker is
Start with **[What Is Passive Dynamic Walking?](/intro/)** — the problem it solves
(legged robots burning through batteries), why gravity-powered walking is possible at
all, and where curved feet fit into that story. Ends by pointing you toward either
building something with your hands, or the research directly.

### I want to build something with my hands, no CAD required
Go to **[Build a Simple Passive Walker](/build-a-toy/)** — cardboard, a pin, and a
tiltable ramp. No 3D printer, no equations, just enough to feel why this works before
looking at any of the research below.

### I already know PDW, or I want the actual research
Go straight to **[The Research](/research/)** — a hub page that lets you jump to
whichever piece you actually want: the physical design, the math, the experiment, the
data, or the CAD files, in any order.
