# The Research: Toroidal Foot Curvature

This is the actual research, broken into six pieces. They were done in roughly this
order — design, then math, then experiment, then results — but you don't have to read
them that way. Pick whichever one answers the question you actually have.

**[How the Robot Was Built](/research/design/)**
The physical robot: toroidal feet, the swappable-foot mechanism, bearings, how yaw was
solved. Start here if you want to know what was actually built before anything else.
→ leads into the math or the CAD files.

**[The Math: Predicting Rocking Period from Curvature](/research/derivation/)**
The closed-form derivation relating frontal radius $R_f$ to rocking period, from energy
conservation to the final formula, plus how good the small-angle approximation actually
is. Start here if you want the theory before the hardware.
→ leads into how that prediction was tested.

**[How We Tested It](/research/experiment/)**
The ramp, the trial protocol, exactly how measurements were taken — and a real
limitation of the method, stated plainly. Read this before Results if you want to know
how much to trust the numbers.
→ leads into the data itself.

**[What We Found](/research/results/)**
The actual data: rocking period vs. $R_f$, the sagittal-radius findings, and the
speed/step-length gait regimes. Jump straight here if you just want the findings.
→ leads into what the data doesn't settle.

**[What We Learned the Hard Way](/research/lessons/)**
Practical lessons and open questions that only showed up from actually building and
running the thing, not from the theory alone.

**[Foot Geometry & CAD Files](/research/cad/)**
The exact toroidal surface equation and the actual model files. Go here if you want to
build your own foot rather than read about someone else's.
